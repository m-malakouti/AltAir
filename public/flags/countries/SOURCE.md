# Country and region flags

SVG flags vendored from [lipis/flag-icons v7.3.2](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons/tree/v7.3.2/flags/4x3), under the included MIT license (`LICENSE.txt`). Only the 156 flags used by the exchange currency mapping are included. Assets are served locally; no runtime CDN requests are made.

Flags identify the currency issuer or region, not the trading venue. EUR uses the European Union flag. Shared/regional currencies without one country (ANG, XAF, XCD, XCG, XDR, XOF, XPF) use a neutral globe, rather than assigning an arbitrary national flag. Historical currency variants retain their issuing country's flag. Unknown mappings or failed images use the same neutral globe.
