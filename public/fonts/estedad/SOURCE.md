Used for the ambient offline scene's Persian copy. Its compact,
low-contrast sans-serif forms were selected as a Persian companion to Pretendard;
this pairing is a visual design choice, not a claim of an identical typeface.
Bundled locally; no runtime font request is made to an external server.
